# Code of conduct

Hexis is a small security project. People here are trusted with other people's secrets, so we keep the space
calm, honest and respectful.

## What we expect

* Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree about the code, never about the person.
* Be specific and honest. If you are not sure, say so. If something is broken, say what you saw, not what you suspect.
* Give credit. Name the person who found a problem or wrote a fix.
* Respect privacy. Never post another person's data, a real vault, real credentials or private messages.
* Keep security reports private until a fix is out (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## What we do not accept

* Harassment, insults, discrimination, threats or unwelcome sexual attention.
* Publishing someone's private information, or pressuring someone to share it.
* Spam, advertising, or content that has nothing to do with the project.
* Sharing exploit code aimed at real users rather than at a reproduction on a throw-away vault.

## What happens when it goes wrong

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block people who break these rules. Report a
problem through the repository's private reporting form (Security tab) or by contacting a maintainer through their
GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and any space that speaks for the project.
